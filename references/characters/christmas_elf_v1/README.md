## Design notes

- Keep the approved friendly closed-mouth smile, green eyes, blonde hair, pointed ears and red knit cap.
- Balanced pear-shaped body, short padded limbs, horizontal jacket quilting, white fur collar and cuffs.
- Navy jacket, teal trousers, brown belt and curled-toe boots, gold buckle and zipper.
- Requested base colors: skin #EEA88A, jacket #20517A, cap #D9545C. Image lighting and shading change their visible appearance.
- The approved sources take precedence if a detail differs between sheets.
- These generated concept views are visual guides, not dimensionally calibrated blueprints. Align proportions in your modeling tool; use the side view to establish boot depth/curl and settle a consistent throwing hand when rigging.

The PNGs retain their original resolution. Descriptive filenames and this index provide labels without covering the artwork. PROMPTS.md records the exact prompts used for the four new sheets.
