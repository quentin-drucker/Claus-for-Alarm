3) Decision: NO warm-up shipped. A full warm-up removes the gameplay stalls but costs 2-3x their duration (14.6 s vs ~5.3 s fresh;
   2.5 s vs ~1.1 s reload), because it compiles variants round 1 never uses; it would add ~15 s to a fresh load-to-play. A lean
   warm-up (Rookie + ghost + first shell + shot + text) would at best trade ~equal time from round start to match load.
   Next actions (owner/next batch): (a) decide whether a lean, loading-screen warm-up is wanted (moves the round-1 2.2-2.6 s stall
   to load, net ~0); (b) reduce material variants (fewer distinct StandardMaterial feature sets: outline/puff/ring/ghost/ticket
   share unshaded-transparent; snowball marker emission), each variant costs ~1-1.5 s fresh; (c) the 10 s first title draw is the
   largest single cost and is not gameplay: profile it (canvas/UI shaders vs engine defaults) before any optimisation;
   (d) measure on another GPU/browser (Firefox, integrated graphics) — this is one high-end machine.
