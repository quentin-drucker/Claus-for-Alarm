# Fresh-player test (P13-T11) — ~20 minutes

For: you (the observer) and one friend who has never seen the game. Use the latest itch ZIP or hosted build in
Chrome at a normal desktop window size.

## Rules for the observer

- Say only: "This is a tower defence game. Play it as you like. Think out loud." Then stay quiet.
- Don't point, explain or answer "how do I…?" questions. If they are stuck for 60 seconds, write it down,
  then give the smallest hint you can and note that you did.
- Note the time and what happened each time they hesitate, misclick, look puzzled or say "huh".

## After the match: ask, write the answers down

1. What was the goal? 2. What confused you most? 3. What did the letters on some snowballs mean?
4. Was anything too fast, too slow, too easy or too hard? 5. Would you play again?

## Turn notes into tickets

Write one line per confusion in `docs/BUGS.md` under "Fresh-player test", with severity (high = they could not
continue without help; medium = slow/wrong for a while; low = cosmetic). Record round reached, lives left,
total time and how many hints you gave.
